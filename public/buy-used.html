Checking a used item before buying

buyNsell editorial guide · Updated 14 October 2026

A low price is useful only when the item fits your needs and works as expected. Use this campus checklist to decide what to ask and what to inspect before agreeing to a purchase.

Start with the exact item

Ask the seller for the model, edition, size or included accessories. For a textbook, compare the title and edition with your course reading list. For a calculator, check that its functions are permitted for your course or examination. A similar-looking product may not meet your requirement.

Ask whether the photo shows the actual item. If the image does not show connectors, labels or damaged areas, request a clearer photo directly. Treat a listing as a starting point for discussion, rather than a guarantee.

Inspect before exchanging

  • Books: look for missing pages, water damage, loose binding and unreadable markings.
  • Electronics: test power, buttons, display, charging and included accessories. Do not rely solely on a photo of a device switched on.
  • Study equipment: check measurements, moving parts, cracks and whether the item can perform its intended task.
  • Furniture: check stability, joints and whether it fits your room and can be moved safely.

Agree on the exchange

Meet in a public campus place during a convenient time. Confirm the final price, included accessories and pickup arrangement before travelling. Inspect the item before making any payment directly to the seller. buyNsell does not collect money, offer delivery or hold deposits.

Do not share a banking password, OTP or card details to receive or arrange an exchange. If someone applies pressure, changes the item unexpectedly or refuses inspection, you can stop the discussion.

After pickup

Keep your own record of what you agreed. Ask the seller to mark the listing sold so other students do not contact them about an unavailable item. If a listing is misleading or inappropriate, use the feedback form or support email with the product name and the problem.

These are general practical suggestions. buyNsell is an independent student platform and does not guarantee listings or exchanges.
