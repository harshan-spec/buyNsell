Agreeing on a student rental

buyNsell editorial guide · Updated 14 October 2026

Renting can help when you need equipment for a short assignment or project. A clear agreement matters because the item must return to its owner after use. buyNsell shows a rental price per day, week or month; the poster and renter agree on the actual arrangement directly.

Confirm the period and total

Ask when the rental starts and ends, and how partial days or weeks are counted. For example, a listing at ₹50 per day does not tell you whether a Friday evening pickup and Monday morning return counts as three or four days. Agree on the total before collecting the item.

Check whether pickup, accessories and any deposit are included. The website does not set deposit terms or collect deposits. Do not assume a listed rate covers damage, transport or late returns.

Record the starting condition

Inspect the item together and take your own photos with permission. List existing scratches or faults and check that the equipment works. Identify accessories that must be returned, such as chargers, leads, cases or rulers. For electronics, the owner should remove their personal information before handing over the item.

Discuss responsibility

  • Where may the item be used, and may another student use it?
  • Who should be contacted if it stops working?
  • What happens if the return is delayed?
  • How will damage be assessed and any deposit returned?

Write the agreed terms in your conversation so both people can refer to them. If either person is uncomfortable with the arrangement, change it or decline the rental.

Return and close the arrangement

Return the item and accessories at the agreed time. Inspect it together again and confirm completion. If the owner no longer wants rental enquiries, they can close the listing through My listings. This marks it unavailable publicly without deleting its management record.

Meet in a public location and avoid sharing sensitive documents or credentials. Report suspicious posts to the website owner. A listing does not mean that buyNsell has verified the poster or guaranteed the item.

These are general practical suggestions. buyNsell is an independent student platform and does not guarantee listings or exchanges.
