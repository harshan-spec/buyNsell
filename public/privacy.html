Privacy policy

Updated 10 October 2026. This policy explains how buyNsell handles information when you browse, publish a listing or send feedback.

Listings are public

We store product name, price, sale or rental type, rental period, photo, seller name, email, phone number, year and department in Supabase. These listing details are publicly visible so buyers can contact sellers. Only publish contact details you are comfortable sharing.

Browser storage

We store your theme choice and private listing management keys in localStorage on your device. These keys allow editing and deletion without an account. Do not share them. Clearing browser data removes management access. We do not require accounts or email verification.

Feedback

Feedback contains your name, optional email, rating and message. It is stored in Supabase for the website owner and is not publicly readable. Avoid submitting passwords, financial information or other sensitive data.

Advertising and third parties

This website integrates Google AdSense. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, IP addresses and browsing information to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads depending on your consent and settings. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites.

Where required, Google's consent message allows you to accept, decline or manage advertising choices. You can manage personalization at Google My Ad Center, learn how Google uses information at Google's partner sites policy, or opt out of participating third-party personalized advertising at YourAdChoices. Your browser also lets you clear or block cookies.

Vercel hosts this website, Supabase stores data and product photos, and Google Fonts supplies fonts. These providers may process network information needed to deliver their services.

Retention and requests

Listings remain until deleted by a seller or removed by the owner. Feedback is retained for handling reports and improving the website. Uploaded photos may remain after a listing is deleted until the owner removes them. Use the feedback form for privacy questions or deletion requests, identifying the listing and providing a reply email. The owner may need to confirm ownership before acting.
