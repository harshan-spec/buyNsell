Terms & marketplace safety

Updated 10 October 2026. By publishing a listing, you agree to these marketplace rules.

Student listings

Post truthful details and only offer items you own or have permission to sell or rent. You are responsible for your listing, contact details and agreements with other students. Do not publish someone else's personal information without permission.

Prohibited content

Do not post illegal or stolen goods, weapons, drugs, tobacco, adult or sexually explicit material, counterfeit goods, hateful content, scams, threats, or content that infringes another person's rights. Do not upload offensive photos or misleading prices. The owner may remove inappropriate listings.

Direct exchanges

buyNsell is a listing platform. We do not take payments, hold deposits, arrange delivery or guarantee a transaction. Inspect products and agree on price and pickup directly. For rentals, agree on the rental period, return arrangements and responsibilities before handing over an item.

Manage your listing

Mark items sold when unavailable and remove outdated listings. Management keys are saved only in your original browser. If access is lost, contact the owner using feedback and identify the listing.

Report concerns

Use the feedback form to report a listing, privacy problem or website issue. Include the item name and enough detail for the owner to locate it. For immediate danger, contact appropriate local services or campus security.

Advertisements

Advertisements, if shown, come from third parties and do not represent an endorsement of their products by buyNsell. Never click ads to support the website or ask other people to do so.
