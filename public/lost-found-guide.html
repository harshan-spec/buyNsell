Returning a found item safely

buyNsell editorial guide · Updated 14 October 2026

A useful lost-and-found report gives other students enough information to recognise an item while keeping private information and ownership checks safe. Choose Lost & found beside Sell an item, then select whether you lost or found the item.

Choose a useful photo

A photo is required for these reports. Show the item clearly against a simple background. Avoid publishing the contents of a wallet, an ID number, an address, bank details, a phone lock screen with messages, or another student's face. Cover sensitive details before taking the photo.

If you lost an item, use a photo of your actual item when available. Do not use another person's image without permission. If you cannot supply a suitable photo, contact campus lost property or security rather than posting private documents.

Leave room to confirm ownership

A found report should not reveal every identifying detail. Keep a distinctive mark, accessory or item detail private so a claimant can describe it. Ask them to explain where and roughly when they lost it. For a locked device, an owner may be able to unlock it in person; do not ask them to share the passcode with you.

Contact and handover

Your name, phone, email, year and department are visible publicly in the report. Use contact details you are willing to share. Arrange a handover in a public campus place or through campus security. Do not request passwords, payment or a reward as a condition for returning someone else's property.

For bank cards, keys or official identity documents, ask campus security or the relevant issuer about the appropriate return process. Do not publish document numbers to prove you have the item.

Resolve the report

Use My listings in the original browser to mark the report resolved after the item is reunited with its owner. It then disappears from public lost-and-found browsing. You can also edit or delete the report using that browser's private management key.

If a report exposes personal information or looks dishonest, contact buyNsell support with the item name and issue. The owner can investigate; the website does not verify every claimant or guarantee recovery.

These are general practical suggestions. buyNsell is an independent student platform and does not guarantee listings or exchanges.
