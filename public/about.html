About buyNsell

buyNsell is a student marketplace for exchanging used essentials within a college campus. Browse items immediately without an account, then contact a seller to agree on the exchange.

How it works

  1. Browse products, search by name, or choose free finds.
  2. Open a product to see its price, whether it is for rent or direct sale, and the seller's contact details.
  3. Arrange pickup directly. For rentals, agree on duration, return date, condition and any deposit before exchanging the item.

Listing an item

Choose Sell an item, enter the product and contact details, and optionally add a photo. Use a price of zero for giveaways. No registration or online payment is required.

Your name, email, phone, year and department are public. My listings lets you edit, mark sold or delete items from the browser you used to publish. Clearing browser data removes this access.

Lost & found

Use Lost & found beside Sell an item to report an item you lost or found. A photo and your contact details are required. Browse reports in the Lost & found tab and contact the poster directly. Verify ownership before returning an item. Mark the report resolved through My listings once reunited.

Contact & report a problem

Use our feedback form to contact the website owner, report an inappropriate listing, request removal of your details, or suggest improvements. Include the product name and issue. Provide an email if you want a reply. Feedback is stored privately for the owner.

Safe campus exchanges

Meet in a public campus location, inspect the item, and avoid sending advance payments to strangers. buyNsell does not process payments, verify seller identities, or guarantee products.
